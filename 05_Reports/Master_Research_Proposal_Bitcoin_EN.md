### 1. Introduction
Bitcoin is a highly volatile crypto-asset. Traditional investment strategies often fail to capture short-term fluctuations. This research employs a **Big Data Analysis** approach to process historical hourly (High-Frequency) price data over a 5-year period (43,000+ records) to build prediction models that are more accurate and responsive to market dynamics.

### 2. Problem Statements
1. How do LSTM and GRU algorithms perform in terms of accuracy (RMSE & MAE) when predicting Bitcoin prices on a Big Data scale?
2. Which algorithm is more computationally efficient (training time) when handling vast High-Frequency datasets?
3. How do technical indicators (RSI, Volatility) influence the stability and reliability of both models?

### 3. Research Methodology (Big Data Pipeline)
This study follows the **Big Data Analytics Lifecycle**:
1.  **Data Ingestion**: Automatic accumulation of raw data from Binance API (completed, 43k+ rows).
2.  **Data Preprocessing**: Data cleaning, handling outliers, and Large-Scale Normalization (Min-Max Scaling).
3.  **Feature Engineering**: Transforming raw data into technical indicators (RSI, MACD, Moving Averages).
4.  **Modeling**: Implementing **LSTM (Long Short-Term Memory)** and **GRU (Gated Recurrent Unit)** architectures, specifically chosen for their ability to handle long-term dependencies in large sequential datasets.
5.  **Evaluation**: Testing models using RMSE, MAE, and Training Time efficiency.

### 4. Novelty (Contribution to Informatics)
- Utilization of **5-Year High-Frequency Data (Hourly)** to capture micro-market dynamics.
- Integration of multi-dimensional technical features as inputs for Deep Learning models.
- Comparative analysis of computational efficiency in processing datasets with tens of thousands of records.

### 5. Expected Outcomes
- A predictive model with low error rates for Bitcoin price estimation.
- An interactive visualization dashboard to monitor trends and predictions.
- A comprehensive research report fulfilling the requirements for a Master's degree in Informatics.
